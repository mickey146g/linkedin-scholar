    About the numbers

    The h-index here is the largest number h such that h posts have at least h reactions each. The i10-index is the number of posts with at least 10 reactions. These are based on LinkedIn reactions, not academic citations.

    Impressions count how many times a post was shown, not how many people saw it. Reactions include likes and every other reaction type.
